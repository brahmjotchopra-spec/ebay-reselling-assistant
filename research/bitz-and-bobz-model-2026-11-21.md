## Repeatable strategy for our seller account
1. Broad catalogue, but launch in verified stock-controlled batches of 3–10 SKUs rather than copying dozens of random titles.
2. Target small, low-weight, durable, non-electrical and non-liquid goods. A single large Amazon multi-pack can be split into multiple independent physically-held eBay lots if accurately described.
3. Require recent eBay sold evidence for the exact variation; asking prices and a competitor's lifetime total do not prove demand.
4. For each candidate: verify Amazon checkout, price and Locker pickup; buy and collect first, count and weigh packaged goods, secure real product/authorised images and a product-compliant listing.
5. Price to deliver **>=£0.50 net** after source cost, eBay business fees and fee VAT, actual UK postage, packaging, listing cost if applicable and provision for returns.
6. Use existing eBay Inventory API GitHub workflows, one verified physically-held quantity per offer; automate checks, don't invent in-stock or supplier dispatch claims.
7. Monitor conversion, cancellation/return rate and net profit per sale; replenish proven winners only.

## Policy
Buying from Amazon after an eBay order and having Amazon send directly to that eBay buyer is against eBay's retailer dropshipping policy. Use Locker collection and self-dispatch, or an authorised wholesale fulfilment arrangement.
