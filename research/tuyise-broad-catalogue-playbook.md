## Operating plan for the owner's store

1. Search a wide variety of everyday nonrestricted goods. Prioritise small, light, durable, non-fragile goods that realistically fit an Amazon Locker and Royal Mail Large Letter or Small Parcel.
2. Check *actual eBay sold transactions* in Seller Hub Product research (ideally last 30–90 days) before buying. A seller's asking price and number of listed products do not prove demand.
3. Verify exact Amazon listing/variation, checkout total, pickup at a nearby Locker, and stock. **Buy and physically hold items before listing** unless using a bona fide wholesale dropship supplier under an agreement.
4. Fully calculate: eBay selling price – landed Amazon cost – seller business selling fees including regulatory fee, per-order fee and VAT where applicable – Royal Mail postage – packaging – refund reserve = net. Require >= £0.50 net per fulfilment; prefer >= £1.00 after uncertainty. Use current rates, not assumptions.
5. Photograph the actual item, or use seller-authorised image / eBay catalogue images. Do not take someone else's Amazon photos or invent product details with AI renders.
6. Create eBay offers only for quantity physically ready to dispatch. Start with one unit per SKU, update real stock only when verified, keep dispatch time realistic, fulfil quickly.
7. Review sales weekly and replenish only products that demonstrate repeated sales, low returns and positive net contribution. Kill stale, unprofitable SKUs.
