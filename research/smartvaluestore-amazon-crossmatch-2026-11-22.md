# smartvaluestore eBay → Amazon UK exact-match investigation
Research date: 22 November 2026

## Method
Start from the seller's identifiable eBay listings and buyer-feedback references, identify exact brand/model/size/pack variants on Amazon UK, then inspect whether retail Amazon costs allow legitimate eBay resale after collection, postage, packaging, eBay business fees, and a returns reserve. An Amazon product match **does not prove** this seller actually sources it from Amazon.

## Operational outcome
- **0 exact Amazon-to-eBay products with verified positive net margin** from these seller listings at this time.
- No Amazon order placed, no live eBay listings created, no existing live listings edited.
- Only Scrubzz was a strong brand/variant match; retail margins were negative under reasonable postage assumptions.
- User requests Amazon-only sourcing with Amazon Locker collection, low initial funds, and focus on high-volume small goods. To find copyable products, continue to use exact match ASIN + photo + variant, then verify price and collection before creating a sellable offer.
- Don't duplicate another seller's listing photos or wording. Write original titles/photos of exact owned stock.
